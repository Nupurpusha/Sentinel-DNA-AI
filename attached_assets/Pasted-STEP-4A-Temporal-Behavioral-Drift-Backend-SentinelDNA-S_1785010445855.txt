GOAL:
Add a lightweight temporal behavioral drift layer that detects suspicious patterns across sequences of events for each identity.

This should help identify stealthy behavior such as gradual behavioral drift, low-and-slow activity, and lateral movement patterns that may not look highly anomalous as individual events.

IMPORTANT:
Ground-truth `label` and attack type are evaluation-only and must NEVER be used in temporal scoring, temporal reasons, risk calculation, or ranking.

1. TEMPORAL DRIFT CALCULATION
==================================================

Using ONLY existing telemetry and behavioral information, analyze recent events for each identity in chronological order.

Create:

temporal_drift_score: 0–100

Use a small set of meaningful sequence-level signals derived from existing data, such as:

- expansion in distinct resources accessed compared with identity baseline
- unusual increase in activity frequency
- accumulation of multiple behavioral deviations over recent events
- repeated authentication failures
- increasing session-duration deviation
- unusual device/location diversity

Do not fabricate fields.

Keep the calculation lightweight, deterministic and explainable.
