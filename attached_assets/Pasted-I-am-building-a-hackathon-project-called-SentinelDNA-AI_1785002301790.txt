Examples:

BRUTE FORCE:
Many failed authentication attempts from the same source within a short period.

IMPOSSIBLE TRAVEL:
The same identity authenticates from geographically distant locations within an impossible travel time.

CREDENTIAL STUFFING:
One or a small number of source IPs attempt authentication against many different identities with a high failure rate.

LATERAL MOVEMENT:
An identity suddenly accesses unusual systems/resources and follows command/resource sequences outside its historical behavior.

DEVICE SPOOFING:
An identity appears using a device fingerprint inconsistent with its trusted historical devices.

LOW-AND-SLOW EXFILTRATION:
An identity gradually performs unusual off-hours access or increasing resource/data access over multiple events.

INSIDER DRIFT:
An identity's behavior gradually changes over time rather than producing one obvious attack event.
